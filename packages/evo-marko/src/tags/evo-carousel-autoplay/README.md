A rotator: one full-width slide at a time, advancing on a timer, with previous/next controls and a playback button. For a strip of items that never moves on its own, use [`evo-carousel`](../evo-carousel/README.md).

Slides are laid end to end three times so the loop always has somewhere to scroll onto. The two runway copies are `aria-hidden` and `inert`, so slide content must not contain `id` attributes.
